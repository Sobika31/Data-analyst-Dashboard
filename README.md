# 📊 Real Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Real Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize supermarket sales performance. This project provides meaningful business insights into sales, profit, customer behavior, product performance, and payment methods.

The dashboard uses Excel's data analysis and visualization features to transform raw sales data into interactive charts, key performance indicators (KPIs), and dynamic reports.

It helps businesses understand sales trends, identify high-performing product categories, and make better data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the best-performing cities.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Evaluate different payment methods.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

**1. Sales Analysis by City**
- Compares total sales across Delhi, Hyderabad, and Mumbai.
- Helps identify the highest-performing city.

**2. Product Line Analysis**
- Analyzes product performance across different categories.
- Compares unit prices across product lines.

**3. Customer Type & Gender Analysis**
- Visualizes customer distribution based on gender and customer type.
- Helps understand customer purchasing patterns.

**4. Customer Rating Analysis**
- Displays average customer ratings across product categories.
- Helps evaluate customer satisfaction.

**5. Sales by Product Line and Payment Method**
- Compares sales performance across different product categories.
- Analyzes payment methods such as E-wallet, Credit Card, and Cash.

**6. Product Line Contribution**
- Uses a doughnut chart to compare unit price contributions across product categories.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters:

- **Product Line:** Filter results by product category.
- **City:** Analyze sales for individual cities.
- **Branch:** Compare performance across branches A, B, and C.
- **Timeline:** Filter the dashboard based on time periods.

All visualizations respond to the selected filters, making the dashboard interactive and user-friendly.

## 💡 Key Insights
